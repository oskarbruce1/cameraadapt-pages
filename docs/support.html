Before you write

Including these details helps us answer faster:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The camera and lens selected in My Gear.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if something looked wrong.

Common questions

My lens isn't in the list. Email us the exact model name and we'll add it.

Why does my result say "Close match"? Your gear can't reach one part of the original shot — usually the background blur or the framing. The card below the rating explains the workaround.

How do I restore a subscription? Open the Profile tab and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the Apple ID you bought it on.

How do I cancel? Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
